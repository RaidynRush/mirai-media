Mirai Media ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Quick Bytes (@real.quickbyte). This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, stored and deleted.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

Data the app accesses

  • Basic information about the owner's own YouTube channel (channel name and id).
  • The videos the app itself uploaded to that channel (video id, title, privacy status, scheduled publish time).

The app does not access viewers' data, comments, subscribers, analytics, or any other channel.

How the data is used

Only to upload the owner's videos as private, schedule the ones the owner approves, and delete the ones the owner rejects. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

The Google sign-in token and the ids of uploaded videos are stored only on the owner's own private computer. No data is sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party. The app sets no cookies and uses no tracking.

Deletion and revoking access

  • Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions (also security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).
  • When access is revoked, or on request, the stored token and video records are deleted from the owner's computer within 7 days. Stored API data is never kept longer than 30 days without being refreshed or deleted.
  • Videos on YouTube can be deleted at any time in YouTube Studio.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: through the Quick Bytes channel page.
